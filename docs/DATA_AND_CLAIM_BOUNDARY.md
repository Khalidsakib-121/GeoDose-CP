# Data and claim boundary

## External real-data sources

The real external sources are NSW Resources/SEED mine rehabilitation geometry, Digital Earth Australia Landsat Fractional Cover and Water Observations, SILO gridded climate data, TERN Soil and Landscape Grid Release-2 soil products, and Geoscience Australia SRTM-derived terrain data. Exact authorities and access links are in `data/SOURCE_REGISTRY.csv`.

## Derived spatial/EO/context products

The project derives the 90-m analysis grid and graph, annual DEA block summaries, context-complete block tables, 180-m anchored sensitivity support, and terrain slope/aspect/roughness from the external source products. These derived products are project outputs and are not independent upstream datasets.

## MineDoseBench

MineDoseBench is a **semi-synthetic known-truth benchmark**. It retains real NSW geometry, graph structure, pretreatment EO/context information, and frozen split/support structure. Treatment assignment, potential-outcome surfaces, residual/measurement-error stress processes, and oracle target truth are generated by the benchmark DGP. It must not be described as observed NSW treatment history.

## Real NSW applicability study

The real NSW outcome is observed DEA photosynthetic-vegetation change from 2024 to 2025. It is an observed satellite-product target, not latent ecological truth. The frozen public NSW snapshot does not provide an auditable longitudinal continuous rehabilitation treatment consistent with the causal estimand. Consequently M2, M4, and M6 fail closed rather than using a proxy exposure.

## Mapped rehabilitation snapshot

The Stage1/Stage2 mapped-rehabilitation fraction is descriptive snapshot exposure/geometry only. It is not used as Stage6A treatment and is not used as a Stage6A predictor.
