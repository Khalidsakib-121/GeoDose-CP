# Stage5B claim boundary

Stage5B is an empirical evaluation of frozen MineDoseBench. It may support statements about empirical selective coverage, efficiency at matched coverage, refusal, false support, structural exact-vs-sparse agreement, predictor robustness, measurement-error sensitivity and MAUP sensitivity.

It does **not** license:
- exact fixed-location conditional coverage;
- an unconditional individual-treatment-effect claim;
- a claim that conformal calibration repairs C2/C4/C5 identification failure;
- a theorem claim for M4;
- a finite-sample nuisance theorem for the RF/XGBoost estimated track;
- relabelling m64-vs-m32 localization sensitivity as the full N2 omitted-information theorem certificate;
- a full-real-line prediction-set claim outside [-1,1];
- an efficiency claim when coverage is unmatched;
- retuning Stage3F thresholds after MineDoseBench results;
- deleting unfavourable registered scenarios.
